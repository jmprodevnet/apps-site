Personal Assistant

Personal Assistant is a private, self-hosted tool. Its owner uses it to keep tasks, reminders and a read-only view of their own mailboxes in one place.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign up or sign in.

Mail access

The owner connects their own Microsoft and Google mailboxes. The app asks only for permission to read mail. It cannot send, change or delete any message.

The privacy policy describes exactly what the app reads and keeps.
