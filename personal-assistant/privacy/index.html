Privacy Policy

Personal Assistant · Last updated 16 November 2026

Who uses this app

Personal Assistant is a private tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own mailboxes. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What the app reads

With the owner's permission, the app reads these details of messages in the Inbox of each connected Microsoft or Google mailbox:

  • the sender's name and address
  • the subject
  • the time the message was received
  • whether the message is read or unread
  • the short plain-text preview that the provider supplies, up to 255 characters
  • a link that opens the message at the provider

The app does not read full message bodies or attachments, and it does not read folders other than the Inbox.

What the app can do

The app asks only for read-only permission: gmail.readonly at Google and Mail.Read at Microsoft. It cannot send, change, move or delete any message.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

The details listed above are kept on a server that the owner runs. They are deleted after 30 days. Mail itself stays with Microsoft or Google.

The app keeps a sign-in token for each connected mailbox so that it can check for new mail. Each token is stored encrypted.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with anyone, and not used for advertising. No person other than the owner sees it.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect a mailbox in the app at any time. This deletes the stored details and the token for that mailbox. Access can also be removed at the provider, from the security page of the Google or Microsoft account.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
